# Changelog

## 0.4.0 - 2026-11-09

- Sectors: apps, travel, jewellery and weddings, auto, and causes (NGO and civic). Government-exam aspirants in education.
- Compliance: online money games, the CCPA dark-pattern list.
- Channels: Telegram, ONDC, FM radio, local outdoor, Roman-script search, and budget tiers.
- `references/personas.md` and `assets/readout.md`.
- Language QA in the review and the critic.
- Festival dates outside 2026 are `Missing` until a panchang is opened.
- Slash commands in `commands/`.
- `evals/` is now a `claude plugin eval` suite, with agency-critic and cause cases.
- Golden outputs in `examples/`.
- Skill versions removed. `plugin.json` holds the version.
- Broader trigger description.

## 0.3.0 - 2026-11-07

- A `feed-hooks` skill for a hook, a retention loop, and how a feed ranks the next post.
- Eleven open papers in `research/papers/`, with what each one supports in `research/INDEX.md`.
- Covington 2016 and Allcott 2019 stay on disk. Their licenses do not allow the public repo.
- India marketing routes hook and ranking jobs to `feed-hooks`.

## 0.2.0 - 2026-11-07

- Campaign kit, founder and agency modes, and a 30-day founder run.
- Worked examples for how far a draft may go.
- Sector files for D2C, FMCG, commerce, restaurants, education, clinics, property, finance, and B2B.
- Language notes, offer shapes, distribution shelves, WhatsApp threads, performance tests, creator sheets, and brand platforms.
- Compliance gates for alcohol and surrogates, food, cosmetics, children, and property documents.
- A critic and a researcher agent. Pressure scenarios in `evals/scenarios.md`.
- Benchmarks older than twelve months are context, not a current price.

## 0.1.0 - 2026-11-07

First release.

- One skill for brand, product, video, performance, creator, B2B, and idea marketing aimed at Indian audiences and Indian companies.
- Reference files for market, channels, compliance, calendar, craft, plays, and dated benchmarks.
- Templates for a brief, a campaign, a script, and a review.
- A hard stop on cure claims, guaranteed returns, and lines the brief does not support.
