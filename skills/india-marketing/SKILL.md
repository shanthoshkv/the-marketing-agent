# India marketing

India is many markets. Language, the household, the price architecture, and the festival in that state are part of the strategy.

## Read this, then only what the job needs

Read `references/market.md` on every job. Before you deliver, run `assets/review.md`.

| The job needs | Read |
| --- | --- |
| Where to spend or post | `references/channels.md` |
| A claim, a creator, personal data, AI people, health, or money | `references/compliance.md` |
| A date, a festival, or a season | `references/calendar.md` |
| Words, a film, or an idea | `references/craft.md` |
| Product, performance, creators, B2B, or a brand platform | `references/plays.md` |
| A budget, a fee, a CPM, or a market size | `references/benchmarks.md` |

Paths are relative to this skill folder.

## Intake

Deliver the artifact in the same reply. Write `Missing` in any field the user did not give. Do not stop at a question.

Do not invent a brand name, a recipe, an ingredient, a clinical effect, a "free from" line, a delivery area, or a service. Those stay `Missing`.

Language: if the cities sit in one language region, write in that language and label it `Assumption:`. Lucknow and Indore are Hindi unless the brief says otherwise. For Mumbai, Hyderabad, or Bengaluru, the language stays `Missing` until the user picks.

A national buyer ("Indian CA firms", "India") is not a city. Draft for that buyer. Any example city starts with `Assumption:`.

Do not widen a city brief to all of India, and do not switch the language to English, to keep moving.

One question, after the artifact, if place, language, or what the product contains or does is `Missing`.

## Output

Fill the fields of the matching file in `assets/`. Do not copy this skill's instructions into the deliverable.

- The job is still unclear: `brief.md`
- Strategy or media: `campaign.md`
- A film, a Reel, a Short, a YouTube script, or a creator brief: `script.md`

Put the audience in the first lines. Write audience-facing lines in the audience language. Under them, add an English working translation when the user is working in English.

A figure from `references/benchmarks.md` carries its source and month. If the user will publish it, say it has to be re-checked. A price or target you propose starts with `Assumption:`.

## A claim that cannot ship

A cure, a guaranteed return, a guaranteed rank or job, a guaranteed tax outcome, or "no notice" does not go in the script as lines anyone could say.

In the first lines, name the cut in English and the gate in `references/compliance.md`. Do not rewrite the forbidden claim in the audience language. Then fill `assets/script.md` with a version that can be shot, using only facts in the brief. A refusal with no script is unfinished.

This is marketing guidance, not legal advice. Confirm a deadline or a penalty against the official text before you give it to a client.
