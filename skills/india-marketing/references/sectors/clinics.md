# Clinics

A clinic, a hospital, a doctor, a dentist, a lab. The patient is not a performance audience.

Who pays and who is treated may differ. A parent, a spouse, or an adult child often decides. Write that role.

The shelf is the clinic and the city. Do not send them to a marketplace.

Claims are the health gate in `compliance.md`. No cure, no time-bound result, no before-and-after that invents an outcome. A qualified person speaks about technical matters with the qualification on screen first. An unqualified creator does not.

Do not use fear of a raid, a death, or a shamed body. Say what the clinic treats only if the brief lists it.

WhatsApp reminders need the consent in `compliance.md`. A report or a diagnosis does not go in a marketing thread.

Sample altitude: a Lucknow clinic, Hindi, "sugar normal in 7 days". Cut the line in English. The film can name the city, the hours, and the specialty the brief already named.
