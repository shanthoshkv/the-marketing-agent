# Finance

Lending, cards, insurance, investments, a trading course. The gate is the BFSI row in `compliance.md`.

Technical advice needs the real registration on screen first. SEBI registration for investment advice. An unqualified creator stays on generic talk: that the product exists, who it is for, and where to read the document. No guaranteed return, no "safe", no target that implies a promise.

Who signs is often not the person who saw the Reel. A spouse or a parent may stop it. Write that.

The offer is the rate, the fee, or the cover the brief states, plus the condition attached to it. If the condition is `Missing`, do not quote the rate alone.

WhatsApp and SMS need the consent rules in `compliance.md`. A bought list of PAN holders is not a channel.

Sample altitude: a fintech Reel, "12% guaranteed". Cut it in English. The film can show the app doing the job the brief describes, with the risk line the brief already uses.
