# Jewellery and weddings

Gold, silver, diamonds, bridal wear, a wedding venue, a planner.

Who pays is the parents, often both families. Who wears it is the bride or the groom. Who is asked is the mother, the aunt, and the family jeweller. Trust is earned over generations, so a new brand borrows it from purity and the bill.

The price of gold moves daily. Do not print a rate. Write "today's rate plus making charge" and leave the figures `Missing`. A making-charge offer ships only as the brief states it. BIS hallmarking with HUID is mandatory for gold jewellery, so "hallmarked" is a baseline, not a differentiator. "Pure" or a karat claim matches the hallmark.

The clock is the wedding muhurats, Akshaya Tritiya, Dhanteras, and the regional wedding season. `calendar.md`. Buying gold on Dhanteras is a ritual in many states, not all.

Bridal looks are regional. A Tamil bride, a Marwari bride, and a Bengali bride do not share one set. Do not cast a generic "Indian bride".

Sample altitude: a Coimbatore jeweller, Tamil, Akshaya Tritiya. Write the temple-jewellery set and the family visit. The rate and any discount stay `Missing`.
