# Apps

A consumer app, a subscription, or software sold to a business. For a GST or CA tool, also read `b2b.md`.

Who pays and who uses can differ: a parent pays for a kid's app, an owner pays for the accountant's tool. Who is asked is a friend who already uses it, or the CA.

The offer is the plan and price in the brief. A free trial says what happens on the last day. UPI Autopay or a card mandate is stated before the user taps. No drip pricing: the price on the ad is the price at checkout, with GST said once. A subscription that is hard to cancel is a dark pattern under the CCPA's 2023 guidelines. See `compliance.md`.

The path is the store page or the web sign-up. A film that cannot show the first screen after install is fame.

Language: the app's interface language. Do not promise Tamil support in the ad if the app is English-only.

Ratings, download counts, and "India's #1" need a dated source.

Sample altitude: a habit tracker, ₹99 a month, Bengaluru students. Language is `Missing` (Bengaluru). Write the first-day screen and the cancel path. Do not invent a streak feature.
