# Property

A builder, a broker, a project. The document is the ad.

Who pays is the buyer. Who is asked is a spouse, a parent, or a CA. The ticket is high. EMI belongs only if the brief names the lender and the total. `offers.md`.

The film and the brochure may say carpet area, price, approval, and possession only when those words are in the brief. The gate is the property row in `compliance.md`. "Only 3 left" ships only when the brief shows the inventory. A tower, a view, or a possession month that is not in the documents stays `Missing`.

Language is the language of the buyer in that city. A Mumbai project is not automatically English. If the brief does not pick, language stays `Missing`. `languages.md`.

Do not use a festival to imply a discount the price list does not have.

Sample altitude: "only 3 flats left" with no inventory in the brief. Cut it. Write the neighbourhood and the configuration the brief did name.
