# FMCG

Soap, a snack, a drink, a staple. The product is already a habit, or it is trying to become one.

The buyer is often the person who runs the kitchen. The user is the household. The kirana owner can refuse the SKU. Put that objection in the plan when general trade is the shelf.

Packs are in `offers.md`: the entry unit and the family pack, only at prices the brief charges. Distribution is in `distribution.md`. A national television idea still needs a state cut when the festival is regional. The calendar says which occasion.

The film is the pack in a real kitchen of that state, not a stock festive set. Language is in `languages.md`.

Do not claim "no chemicals", a health effect, or a comparison the brief cannot show. Food and health gates are in `compliance.md`.

Sample altitude: a detergent in coastal Karnataka, monsoon, Kannada. Write the wash in that season. Do not write a Hindi line and call it local.
