# Auto

A two-wheeler, a car, an EV, a dealership, a used-vehicle seller, a service centre.

Who pays is the earner, often with a loan. Who rides may be a son, a daughter, or the whole family. Who is asked is the mechanic, the cousin who owns one, and the dealer.

The offer is the price the brief gives. Say ex-showroom or on-road, never leave it ambiguous. EMI only with the lender and the total, `offers.md`. A state or central EV subsidy ships only as the brief states it. Schemes change. Otherwise `Missing`.

Mileage and range are the certified test figures (ARAI or the brief's source), labeled as test figures. Do not promise real-world range. Do not show unsafe riding, no helmet, or a stunt without a "professional rider" super.

The clock is Navratri and Dhanteras deliveries, the harvest season in rural markets, and the new financial year for fleets. `calendar.md`.

Sample altitude: a 125cc scooter for women commuters in Jaipur, Hindi. Write the commute and the boot space if the brief gives it. Mileage stays `Missing` without a certified figure.
