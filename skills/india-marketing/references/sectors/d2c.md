# D2C

A brand selling its own product to a household, usually online or on WhatsApp, often without a national distributor.

Who pays, who uses, and who is asked are often three people. The feed introduces the brand. A family member or a friend closes it. Trust breaks on a bad product before it breaks on an ad. The evidence is in `benchmarks.md`. Do not restate the percentages here.

The offer is a rung from `offers.md` that the brief already charges. The shelf is the site, WhatsApp, or a marketplace from `distribution.md`. If quick commerce is not live in that city, leave it out.

The film shows the pack, the price, and the use in that home. Fame if the path is `Missing`.

Claims follow `compliance.md` for the category of the product. A snack does not become a health product in the caption.

Sample altitude: women in two Hindi cities, ₹299, Diwali, contents unknown. Write the guest plate and the price. Do not name a flavour.
