# Travel

A hotel, a homestay, a tour, a pilgrimage package, a bus or a flight seller.

Who pays is often the eldest earner. Who decides is the family, and for a pilgrimage, the elders. Who is asked is a relative who went last year.

The offer is the package price in the brief, with what it includes: stay, meals, transfer, darshan pass. Say per person or per room. Taxes are in the shown price or said next to it. "Lowest fare" needs a dated comparison.

The clock is school holidays, long weekends, the pilgrimage season, and the wedding season for venues. Dates are in `calendar.md`. A yatra date moves with the panchang. Confirm it.

Do not promise a view, the weather, a darshan time, or a visa outcome. Photos are of the actual property, or labeled. A synthetic room is a synthetic room. `compliance.md`.

Sample altitude: a Rishikesh riverside stay for Delhi families, ₹4,500 a night. Write the drive time and the weekend. Meals and rafting stay `Missing` unless the brief includes them.
