# Causes

An NGO, a fundraiser, a public-health or civic campaign, or an idea that asks people to change a habit. The ask is a behaviour, a donation, or a signature, not a purchase.

Who gives is often not who is helped. Who is asked is the family WhatsApp group, the office, or the temple or gurdwara committee. Trust is the product. Show the work, the place, and the people doing it, only where the brief has them.

The offer is the ask in the brief: an amount, a volunteer slot, a pledge. A tax line (80G) ships only if the brief says the organisation holds that certificate. Foreign money needs FCRA registration. If the brief takes donations from abroad and does not mention FCRA, mark it `Missing`.

Non-partisan civic information can ship: how to register, where to go, what the rule is. A party, a candidate, or a line that tells people whom to vote for does not. `compliance.md` refuses election persuasion and communal creative. A cause film that names a community as the problem is communal creative.

Do not use a child's face or a patient's face without consent stated in the brief. Do not use poverty as spectacle. The person helped speaks in their own words or not at all.

Numbers ("10,000 meals") ship only when the brief proves them, with the year.

Sample altitude: a Pune blood-donation drive on a Sunday. Write the place, the hour, and what a first-time donor should eat before. The hospital partner is `Missing` until the brief names it.
