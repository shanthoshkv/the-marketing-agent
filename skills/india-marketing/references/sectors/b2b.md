# B2B

Software, a service, a tool sold to a firm. The GST billing case in `examples.md` is the altitude.

Draw the set: who signs, who uses it, who can block it. In a CA firm that is the partner, the article, and the client who will not take another login. In a factory it may be the owner, the accountant, and the storekeeper.

The clock is the buyer's calendar. Filing week, month end, renewal, March. Not a festival, unless the product is also a gift. `calendar.md`.

Proof is a situation the brief contains. Do not invent a customer logo, an hour saved, or a feature. "Prepare" is not "we file" unless a licensed workflow is in the brief. The tax-software row is in `compliance.md`.

Price in INR. GST on the invoice when you state a price. Channels: search, LinkedIn, a study circle, opt-in WhatsApp. The thread is in `whatsapp.md`. National TV and IPL are the wrong first buy.

Language: English for a professional console is fine. The owner's screen and the WhatsApp reply follow the firm's language. If the brief has no state, owner language stays `Missing`.

Sample altitude: "guaranteed no notice" for a billing tool. Cut it. Write the filing-week desk and the tool they will not rip out on day one, if the brief says that tool exists.
