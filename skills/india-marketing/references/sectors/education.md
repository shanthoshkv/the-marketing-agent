# Education

Coaching, a school, a college, a course, an exam product. The clock is admissions and exams, not Diwali, unless the course is a gift. Dates of the season are in `calendar.md`. Do not joke about results.

Who pays is often a parent. Who uses it is the student. Who is asked is a teacher, an older sibling, or a friend who sat the exam.

The offer is the fee in the brief. EMI only if the brief names the lender and the total. `offers.md`.

The claim gate is the education row in `compliance.md`. Cut a guaranteed rank, selection, mark, placement, or government job. A proved, current result can be said as that result, with the year and the count. Do not turn one student's story into "everyone clears".

Language is the language the parent will trust. For a high-consideration course that is often the state language, not Hinglish. `languages.md`.

Sample altitude: a JEE class in Kota that wants "rank under 100 guaranteed". Cut that line in English. The film can say who teaches and when the batch starts, if the brief says so.
