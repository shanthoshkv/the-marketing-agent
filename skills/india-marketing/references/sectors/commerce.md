# Commerce

Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho, Blinkit, Zepto, Instamart, and the brand's own listing.

The page and the film have to match the listing that is live: title, variant, pack, price. If the city is not served, the quick-commerce app stays out. Rules for each shelf are in `distribution.md`.

Sale events (Great Indian Festival, Big Billion Days, and the rest) are a retail calendar. They sit beside the state festival in `calendar.md`. They do not replace Onam, Puja, or Pongal.

The creative job is search and the pack shot. A lifestyle film that hides the pack is fame, and it should be labeled fame.

Do not invent a rating, a review count, or a "lowest price". Those are claims. If they are not on the live page, they stay `Missing`.

Sample altitude: a listed pack in Indore on one app, not listed in Lucknow. The Indore cut can name the app. The Lucknow cut cannot.
