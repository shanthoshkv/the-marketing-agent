# Restaurants

A restaurant, a cafe, a cloud kitchen, a sweet shop. The product is a meal or a box the brief describes. Do not invent the menu.

Who decides is the person ordering and the people who will eat. A festive box for guests is a different job from lunch for one.

The shelf is the outlet, the delivery app the brief names, or WhatsApp. Pins and a cutoff are a service. If the brief does not have them, do not promise "before puja". `distribution.md` and `whatsapp.md`.

The film shows the food the brief named, the price, and the neighbourhood. Lucknow and Indore do not share a shopfront.

Food claims, including "immunity" and "no sugar" used as a health claim, follow `compliance.md`. A sweet is not a medicine.

Sample altitude: a Diwali box at a price the brief states, contents `Missing`. Write the occasion and the price. Do not name the sweets inside.
