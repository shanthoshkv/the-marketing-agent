# Offers

The price in the brief is the price. Any other rung starts with `Assumption:` and is a proposal, not a fact.

## Shapes that fit Indian buying

Use the shape the category already uses. Do not invent a second price to make the offer look clever.

| Shape | What it is | Use it when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Entry pack | A small unit at a round rupee price the brief already charges | Trial, kirana, impulse |
| Family pack | The size the household finishes | The user is the home, not one person |
| Combo | Two things the brief already sells, in one pack | Gifting, a festival, a basket |
| Festive hamper | A gift pack with a known end date | The contents are in the brief |
| Second unit | A stated price for the second piece | The brief has that price. A host who keeps one and gives one |
| EMI | A monthly split of a ticket the brief names | Phones, education fees, durables. The lender and the total are in the brief |
| UPI | The way they pay, not a discount | Almost any close |
| COD | Cash on delivery | Trust is low and the business can carry returns. Say the return cost is an assumption if you do not have it |

A free gift, a "today only" price, or a coupon needs an end and a real stock of the gift. Otherwise it is a dark pattern. The rule is in `compliance.md`.

## What the film shows

A conversion film shows the rung the viewer can buy, the pack, and where to get it. If the path is `Missing`, label the film as fame and do not say "order now".

GST on a B2B invoice is visible when you quote a software price. If the price is `Missing`, do not invent a plan named Starter or Growth.
