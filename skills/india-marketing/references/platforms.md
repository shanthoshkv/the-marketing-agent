# Platforms

A platform is one tension the brand can say for a year. It is not a festival post.

## Shape

- The tension, in one sentence, from the buyer's week. A host who will not serve a cheap box and cannot carry a costly one. A CA whose staff retypes bills. A tenant who cannot tell if the carpet area is real.
- The line a person will still understand in a year.
- Three places it shows up: the film, the pack or the product screen, and the sentence a seller can say.
- What the brand will not say. Claims from `compliance.md`. Communities it will not use as a joke.

A platform that only works in English, in Mumbai, is a Mumbai platform. Say so, or write the other language as its own idea. `languages.md` is the register.

## Topical work

A brand that answers the ordinary news of the week can do that only if it already has that permission. Do not attach the brand to a tragedy, an election, or a community for a week's reach.

Do not lift a famous Indian campaign line and change the brand name. Describe the structure. Write a new line from this brief.

## Sample altitude

Brief: a snack at ₹299 for guests in two Hindi cities. The tension is the plate that has to look like a gift and still be a price the host can repeat. The line is about the plate being ready. The brand name is `Missing`, so the line does not go on a pack in this buy.
