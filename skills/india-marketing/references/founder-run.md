# Founder run

Founder mode is one business and, unless they named more, one city. The output is the short kit: the idea, three hooks, one script, and a WhatsApp reply only if they have opt-in. Agency mode is the full `assets/kit.md` plus `assets/critic.md`.

## A 30-day run

Use this when they ask for a month of content. Ship eight slots. The days between are replies and reshoots of the hook that worked. Do not write twenty-two extra claims to fill a calendar.

| Slot | Day | What ships |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 1 | The idea, in the audience language. No new claim. |
| 2 | 3 | Hook A, as a post or a story |
| 3 | 7 | Hook B |
| 4 | 10 | Hook C |
| 5 | 14 | The film from `assets/script.md` |
| 6 | 18 | The same film, the winning hook only |
| 7 | 24 | The path to buy, only if `distribution.md` has a real shelf. Otherwise a question to the owner |
| 8 | 28 | One proof the owner actually has: a pack, a shop, a bill, a room. Not a made-up review |

If a festival in `calendar.md` falls inside the month and it is that city's festival, slots 5 and 6 use that occasion. If none does, do not paste a festival on the month.

Each slot repeats facts from the brief. A slot does not introduce an ingredient, a rank, a cure, or a discount that was `Missing` on day 1.

## What the founder does not get

A media plan with invented CPM. A creator roster with invented fees. A second city. Those are agency mode, and only when the brief asks for them.
