# Channels

Recommend a channel only for the job it does in India. Say what you are not buying, and why.

TikTok is not an India media channel. It has been banned since June 2020. Short video here means Instagram Reels, YouTube and Shorts, Moj, Josh, and ShareChat.

| Channel | Use it for | Leave it out when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| YouTube and Shorts | Search, reviews, tutorials, and long trust. Shorts for the same idea, cut down. | The offer expires tonight and you have no audience yet. |
| Instagram and Facebook | Reels, Stories, social commerce, Click-to-WhatsApp. Meta is where a large share of social discovery sits. | You need a regional-language town and you have not checked that this placement actually reaches it. |
| WhatsApp | The relationship after the click: catalogue, order reply, filing-week note, delivery update. People treat it as personal. | You do not have opt-in. Bulk promotional sends without purpose-specific consent do not go in the plan. See `compliance.md`. |
| Moj, Josh, ShareChat | Regional short video and, on ShareChat and Moj, free microdrama. Advertising is the reported money path, not subscriptions. | The brief is English-only B2B. |
| LinkedIn | Founders, CAs, hiring, and B2B proof. | A household FMCG film. |
| TV, CTV, cinema | Fame, a regional feed, a match or a release the audience already gathers for. | A ₹2 lakh test budget. |
| Regional print | Trust and festival editions in a named city. | You needed performance CPA this week. |
| OOH and DOOH | A corridor, a festival week, a store neighbourhood. | You cannot name the road. |
| Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho, quick commerce | Retail media and the page where the buy happens. | The SKU is not listed in that city. |
| Search | People already typing the problem: "GST invoice format", "e-invoice error", the category in their language. Many type Hindi in Roman script, or speak the query. Write the keyword list both ways. | You are launching an idea nobody searches for yet. Use it later. |
| Telegram | Exam prep, trading education, and deal channels people join on purpose. | The brief is a household product, or the category is investment advice without a SEBI registration. |
| ONDC | A seller who wants buyer apps beyond one marketplace, often grocery and food in named cities. | The SKU or the city is not live on a buyer app. Check before you plan it. |
| FM radio | Drive-time reach in one city, a store opening, a local offer, in the city's language. | You need a click this week. |
| Auto-rickshaw backs, bus shelters, wall paint | One neighbourhood, a town, or a rural belt where a wall is the billboard. | You cannot name the route or the village. |

## How a typical path fits together

Discovery is often the feed or search. The decision is often a person, a CA, or a retailer. The close is often WhatsApp, the marketplace page, or the shop.

A conversion film that cannot say the price, the pack, and where to get it is a fame film. Label it as fame.

Click-to-WhatsApp is a media objective and an operations promise. Write the first reply the business will actually send. If nobody can answer in that language by that hour, do not buy the clicks.

## Budget tiers

The tier sets how many channels, not which idea. Name the tier in the plan. A budget the user did not give is `Missing`, and the tier you assume starts with `Assumption:`.

| Monthly money (INR) | Mix |
| --- | --- |
| Under ₹50,000 | One feed (Reels or Shorts), WhatsApp replies, Google Business Profile, and the founder on camera. No creator fee, no print. One hook test. |
| ₹50,000 to ₹5 lakh | One feed plus search, two or three micro-creators or barter, Click-to-WhatsApp if someone can reply. Two cities at most. |
| ₹5 lakh to ₹50 lakh | Two feeds, search, a creator roster, retail media if listed, a regional print or FM burst for a festival. |
| Above ₹50 lakh | Add CTV, OOH in named corridors, and a regional TV or cinema flight where the audience gathers. Agency mode. |

Rates for any of these come from `benchmarks.md` with the source and month, or stay `Missing`.

## B2B

The GST and compliance calendar beats a brand-awareness burst. Useful surfaces are search templates, a filing-week WhatsApp note to firms that opted in, LinkedIn proof, study circles, and a partner who will take a peer call. National TV and IPL are the wrong first buy for a practice tool.
