# Craft

## The film

Write the hook in the viewer's language. The first two seconds earn the rest. On-screen text is part of the script, not a subtitle someone may add later. Many people watch in a noisy room, on a shared phone, with the sound low.

Lengths that match the job:

- About 15–30 seconds when the job is a response or an order.
- About 45–90 seconds when the job is consideration.
- About 8–15 minutes when YouTube is a review, a lesson, or a proof story.

A conversion film shows the price, the pack, and where to get it. A fame film does not pretend to be one.

Casting and rooms match the state. Lucknow and Indore can share a structure. They do not automatically share a skyline, a sweet, or a neighbourhood. Faces, clothes, and shops should look like those cities, not like a stock "Indian festival" set.

Disclosure, if anyone was paid or the picture is synthetic, sits inside the film as `compliance.md` describes. Build it into the timed script.

## Hinglish and Hindi

Fluent Hinglish sounds like speech:

> Mehmaan bees minute mein hain. Do hazaar ka box bhaari padta hai. ₹299 mein dena banta hai.

Mock pidgin does not ship:

> Me Indian housewife very happy. Diwali snack buy now ji. Best quality guaranteed.

Use the second only as a private check. Never put it in a script, a caption, or a deck the client might read aloud.

Hindi supers are short enough to read once:

> मेहमान आ गए। ₹299। दूसरा डिब्बा घर के लिए।

If you write the spoken line in Hindi or Hinglish, add the English working translation underneath when the user is working in English. Do not replace the spoken line with the translation.

## The idea

One tension the brand can keep for a year. Examples of the shape, not lines to reuse: the gift that does not embarrass the giver; the bill that matches the return; the regional festival the national plan forgot.

Topical jokes are for brands that already have that permission. Do not attach the brand to a tragedy, an election, or a community for a week's reach.

## A still, when the user asks for one

Fill this. Do not default to marigolds, a spice pile, or a tricolour unless the product is those things.

- City and room: a drawing room, a shop, a clinic, a classroom. Name the city. Lucknow and Indore do not share a room.
- Time of day and light: window, shopfront, or the bulb that is already there. No unexplained golden haze.
- Clothes that a person in that city would wear for this occasion. Not a costume.
- Pack: the real pack. Words on it in the script from `languages.md`. If the pack does not exist yet, say the pack is `Missing` and do not design a logo.
- What is absent: no monument, no extra brand, no child delivering the line, no fake "only 3 left" sticker.

One still is one city. A second city is a second still.

## What to cut

Cut a line that could be said of any brand in any country. Cut a festival that is not this audience's festival. Cut a claim the brief does not support. Cut a joke that needs a community to be the punchline.
