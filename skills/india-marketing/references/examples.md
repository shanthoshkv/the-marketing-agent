# Examples

These show how far to go. They are not copy to reuse. The brands, packs, and features below are invented on purpose so you can see what stays `Missing`.

## A snack, two cities

Brief: a ₹299 snack, women in Lucknow and Indore, a Diwali Reel and a media sketch.

Write this. Hindi, labeled `Assumption:`. Two cuts, one structure. Lucknow rooms in the Lucknow cut, Indore rooms in the Indore cut. The job in the house is the plate for guests. The price ₹299 is on screen. The path to buy is `Missing`, so the film is fame, not an order film. No WhatsApp, no app, no "order now".

Do not write this. A brand name, a flavour, "no maida", a second price, a pin code, or a creator. Diwali can be the occasion. Confirm the date. Do not play the film for Chhath or Christmas.

## A GST product, no city

Brief: a GST billing SaaS for Indian CA firms and small business owners.

Write this. Two buyers. The partner signs, the article uses it, the owner sends the bill. Filing week is the clock, not Diwali. Tally or Excel can stay. The line is "prepare the bill", because the brief does not say the product files the return. CA-facing lines can be English. Owner language is `Missing` until a state is named. Any city you suggest starts with `Assumption:`.

Do not write this. A price, e-invoice, e-way bill, "no notice", or a national TV plan.

## A powder and a cure

Brief: a Hindi YouTube creator must say the powder cures diabetes in 7 days.

Write this. The cut, in English, in the first lines, with the health gate. Then a short Hindi film: it is sponsored, it is a powder, it is not a medicine. No clinic, no white coat, no number of days.

Do not write this. The cure in Hindi or in any other language. An ingredient, a dose, a taste, or a price. Those are `Missing`.
