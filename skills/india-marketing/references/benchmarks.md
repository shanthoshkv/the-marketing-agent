# Benchmarks

Vintage: November 2026. Re-check any figure before it is published or put in a client deck. Bands disagree. Quote the source and the month in the same sentence as the number. Never collapse these into one "average" rate or one market size.

If a newer primary source conflicts with a row, use the newer source and say you did.

## Advertising market

- Dentsu–e4m Digital Advertising Report, as reported February 2026: digital advertising closed 2025 at ₹71,621 crore. Social was about 29% (₹21,057 crore). Online video was about 28% (₹20,004 crore), projected to reach about 29% of digital by the end of 2026. A companion report forecast digital at ₹84,977 crore in 2026 and about 70% of total ad spend by 2027.
- WPP Media This Year Next Year, as reported February 2026: Indian advertising about ₹2,01,891 crore in 2026, up 9.7%. Digital, including digital extensions of other media, about 68.1%. Commerce-led advertising (retail media, quick commerce, social commerce) was the fastest reported grower at about 24.2%.

These two reports measure different totals. Do not add them together.

## Language, trust, and cities

- WPP Media and Kantar Rural Barometer, October 2026, rural mobile users across 12 state groupings: more than 83% of rural adults said local-language content is essential. Hindi was the most watched language at 63.9%. 46.9% watched in their own regional language, 55.0% among women. 52.6% used at least one AI feature (60.4% of rural Gen Z).
- Kantar and DB Corp, Urban Bharat, July 2026: affluent population in Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities up 76% over six years. NCCS A households in those markets from 26% to 40%.
- MSL India and Youth Ki Awaaz, November 2026, 3,509 people under 30 in Tier 2, 3, and 4 cities: 73.1% trust family and close friends most before buying something new. Trust breaks first on a bad product experience (59.6%), then greenwashing (35.7%), then poor service (32.8%).
- Affle Festive Pulse 2025, via Financial Express, October 2025: 79% of users engage better with ads in their local language. 70% of marketers were reported running state-specific campaigns. Regional-language creators were reported 15–30% above off-season rates during the festive period. This row is older than the 2026 studies.

## A single-source commerce claim

BW Marketing World, 7 November 2026, one trade article: 72% of product discovery in India happens on WhatsApp; social drives 77% of retail brand and product discovery, of which Instagram and Facebook account for 96%. Treat this as that article's claim. It is not a government or industry-body census.

## Creators

Published 2026 Reel prices inside the same follower band differ by about two times. Quote a band. Do not quote a single "India rate".

Roster list prices, Digital Berries, October 2026, 810 published Instagram Reel rates on their talent network:

| Tier | Followers | Median Reel | Typical range they published |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Micro | 10K–100K | ₹40,000 | ₹25,000–₹55,000 |
| Mid | 100K–500K | ₹70,000 | ₹50,000–₹91,000 |
| Macro | 500K–1M | ₹1 lakh | ₹69,000–₹1.8 lakh |
| Mega | 1M+ | ₹2 lakh | ₹1 lakh–₹3 lakh |

Lower bands from other 2026 agency and marketplace guides (BrandGrow, CollabVue, CreloAI, and similar cards published mid to late 2026): nano Reels (about 1K–10K) often ₹1,000–₹12,000; micro Reels often a few thousand up to about ₹40,000. Those cards are asking prices and closed deals mixed together. Say which kind you are looking at.

YouTube integrations are often priced above a Reel at the same size. Finance, health, and tech are priced above beauty and lifestyle in these cards. Ads usage and exclusivity are often negotiated at roughly +30% to +100% on the content fee. GST at 18% is usually extra. None of these add-ons are a statute. They are how quotes are built.

Follower count is the wrong primary number. Ask for average views, comment quality, and the share of the audience in the cities you are buying.

## What these files will not give you

A CPM, a CPA, or an ROAS for the user's category. Those move by week, city, and objective. If you need one, look it up for that account and label the date. Do not invent one from this page.
