# Calendar

Plan the year from the audience's calendar. Confirm festival dates against that year's panchang before anyone books media. Dates move. Communities in the same city do not always share a night.

A festival gets its own idea, built from what people do then. A Diwali film with new supers is not an Onam film, a Pongal film, or a Durga Puja film.

## How to weight a year

1. State peaks for the states you are actually in.
2. National commerce peaks, which still play out differently by city.
3. The category's own clock: GST due dates, school terms, monsoon, wedding muhurats, harvest.

Name the states you are not covering. Silence is better than a pan-India "festive" line.

## Diwali is a season

Treat it as phases, not a burst on the night. A starting shape: warm-up while people are in the mood and not yet buying, build while they are choosing, peak while they are checking out, a tail into weddings and Bhai Dooj, and a reserve you can move to whatever is working. Book earlier than the peak. Inventory tightens, and a regional festival such as Onam has already happened weeks before a national Diwali plan usually starts.

| Diwali | Sunday 8 November |
| Chhath | 15 November |

Bhai Dooj is two days after Diwali. Confirm it. Christmas does not inherit this film.

For any year other than 2026, a festival date is `Missing` until someone opens that year's panchang or the state's holiday list. Do not shift 2026 dates by a year. Lunar festivals move by up to three weeks. The `marketing-researcher` agent fetches the date and tags the page.

## Who sits it out

- A tax or billing product sits out Diwali unless it is also a gift. Its clock is the due date.
- A coaching brand sits out a joke during board, JEE, and NEET weeks.
- A clinic does not borrow a festival to imply a cure.
- A builder does not invent a festive price.
- Chhath is not a Diwali extension. Christmas does not inherit a Diwali film.
- A brand with no shop in Kerala does not buy Onam media there.

## Category clocks that beat festivals

- GST and tax products: the due-date week, not Diwali, unless the product is also a gift.
- Education: admission and exam months.
- Agriculture-linked brands: the harvest in that state, not a national festive template.
