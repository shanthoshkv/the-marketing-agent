# Performance

One offer. One next step. Hook tests before audience tests. Do not invent a ROAS, a CPA, a CPM, or a conversion rate. `benchmarks.md` does not have those for the user's account.

## A seven-day test in two cities

Use this when the brief names two cities and one film.

- Two cuts. Each city sees only its own cut.
- Three hooks. Same film after the first line. The hooks are in `languages.md`'s language for that city.
- Spend is `Missing` until the user gives a number. If you propose a ceiling, start it with `Assumption:`.
- The next step is the one the business can fulfil. A WhatsApp click needs the reply in `whatsapp.md` and a person who answers. If nobody answers, do not buy the click.
- Measure what the business can see: replies answered, orders confirmed, invoices sent, shop visits. Views are a footnote.

## Kill rule

Write the rule you chose, and label it as your rule. It is not a platform benchmark.

A usable shape: stop a city after a stated rupee amount if fewer than a stated share of impressions reach the price super. Pick the amount and the share with the user, or mark both `Assumption:`.

Each city stands alone. Do not move a losing city's money to a third city the brief did not name.

## COD and prepaid

COD can be the right close when the buyer does not trust the brand yet. It costs returns. If you do not have the return cost, say the economics are `Missing` and do not recommend COD as free.
