# Distribution

This file is where the product sits. `channels.md` is where the ad runs. Do not put a shelf in the plan unless the brief says the SKU is there, in that city.

| Shelf | The film and the page must | Leave it out when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Kirana and general trade | The pack a shopkeeper can recognise, the rupee price, the margin story only if the brief has it | You have no distributor and no shop list |
| Modern trade | The pack on a shelf a person in that city would walk past | The chain is not named |
| Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho | The title a person would search, the pack shot, the variant that is actually listed | The listing is `Missing` |
| Blinkit, Zepto, Instamart | The city, the pack, the promise only inside the pins they deliver | The SKU is not live in that city |
| Own site or WhatsApp | The reply in `whatsapp.md`, and a person who can answer | There is no opt-in |
| A clinic, a classroom, a CA's office | The place the buyer already goes | You are sending them to an app they did not ask for |

Marketplace sale events are a retail calendar. They sit beside the state festival. They do not replace it. Dates and weight are in `calendar.md`.

A delivery promise ("tonight", "before puja") is a service. If the brief does not name the pins and the cutoff, the promise stays `Missing`.
