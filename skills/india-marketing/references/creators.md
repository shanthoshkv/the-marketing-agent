# Creators

Pick language, city, and average views before follower count. A large account with few viewers in the cities you are buying is the wrong buy.

Fee bands live only in `benchmarks.md`. Quote a band and the source. Do not collapse them into one India rate. Usage rights, exclusivity, and GST are extra lines on the quote. In a festival week, say that regional creators book early. Do not invent this week's rate.

## The sheet you can send

- Creator: language, city, why this person. `Missing` if you have not been given a name.
- They may say: only the lines in the script.
- They may not say: the cuts named in the first lines of the job. No cure, no guaranteed rank, no "only 3 left", no ingredient the brief does not contain.
- Disclosure: the word from `compliance.md`, in the language of the film, spoken at the start and held on screen. You put it in the brief. You do not leave it for later.
- Usage: organic only, unless the brief buys ads rights. Whitelisting and edits are in writing before anyone boosts the film.
- Fee: the band, the source, the month. Add a line for ads rights and a line for exclusivity. Mark both `Missing` if the deal does not have them.

## Do not

Buy on follower count. Write the creator's script in English and ask them to "make it regional". Use a child as the person who sells a health, money, or education product. Use a synthetic face of a real person. The synthetic rules are in `compliance.md`.
