# Plays

Use the checklist that matches the job. Leave the others unread. Prices and market sizes come from `benchmarks.md`, quoted with source and month, or they are labeled `Assumption:`.

## Product and go-to-market

- Name who pays, who uses, and who is asked.
- Write the pack and price ladder the brief supports. Mark any rung you invented.
- Name where it is bought this month. If the SKU is not on a quick-commerce app in that city, do not put the app in the plan.
- Write the objection in that kitchen, climate, shop, or practice. Water, power cuts, a CA who will not retype bills, a kirana that will not stock a new SKU.
- Sequence: proof, first city, the offer, the path to reorder. A national logo map comes after a city works.
- Competitors are the ones a buyer in that city actually compares. Name them only when you know them. Do not invent a feature list for a rival.

## Video

- One job: fame, consideration, or a response.
- Three hooks in the audience language. They share a film and change the first line.
- Timed script from `assets/script.md`: picture, spoken line, super, disclosure.
- End card: price, city, and the next action the business can fulfil.
- Say what you will not shoot.

## Performance

- One offer, one next step. A WhatsApp thread needs the exact first reply.
- Test hooks before you test audiences.
- COD versus prepaid is an economics choice. State the assumption. Do not invent a ROAS, a CPA, or a conversion rate.
- Measure the thing the business can see: chats answered, orders confirmed, invoices sent, shop visits. Vanity views are a footnote.
- Kill rule: write the number that stops a creative, and label it as a rule you chose, not as a platform benchmark.

## Creators

- Pick language, city, and average views before follower count. A large account with few Indian viewers is the wrong buy.
- Fee bands are in `benchmarks.md`. They disagree. Quote a band and the source. Usage rights for ads, exclusivity, and GST sit on extra lines.
- The brief contains the disclosure, the claims they may say, and the claims they may not.
- Whitelisting and edits are in writing before anyone boosts the film.
- In festival weeks, regional creators get booked early. Say so. Do not invent this week's rate.

## B2B and company

- Draw the buyer set: the person who signs, the person who uses it, the person who can block it. In a CA firm that is often the partner, the article staff, and the client who refuses another login.
- Proof is a named Indian situation: filing week, e-invoice errors, Tally or Excel still in the corner. Coexist with the tool they will not rip out on day one.
- Price in INR, annual if that matches how practices buy. GST on the invoice is visible.
- Channels from `channels.md`: search, opt-in WhatsApp, LinkedIn, a study circle. The campaign clock is the compliance calendar.
- Say what the product does not do, in the ads as well as the contract. "Prepare and validate" is not "we file this for you".
- Case-study shape: the practice, the month-end mess, what changed, the number the partner will say on a call. No number the partner has not agreed to.

## Idea and brand platform

- The tension, in one sentence.
- The line people will still understand in a year.
- Three places it shows up: the film, the pack or the product screen, and the sales line.
- What the brand will not say. Include the claims and the communities it will not use.
- A platform that only works in English, in Mumbai, is a Mumbai platform. Say so, or write the regional version as its own idea.
