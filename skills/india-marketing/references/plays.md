# Plays

Use the checklist that matches the job. Leave the others unread. The long version of each job lives in one file: offers, distribution, performance, creators, platforms, sectors, founder-run. Prices and market sizes come from `benchmarks.md`, quoted with source and month, or they are labeled `Assumption:`. The altitude for a finished answer is in `examples.md`.

## Product and go-to-market

- Name who pays, who uses, and who is asked.
- Write the pack and price ladder the brief supports. Shapes are in `offers.md`. Mark any rung you invented.
- Name where it is bought this month. Shelves are in `distribution.md`. If the SKU is not on a quick-commerce app in that city, do not put the app in the plan.
- Write the objection in that kitchen, climate, shop, or practice.
- Sequence: proof, first city, the offer, the path to reorder. A national logo map comes after a city works.
- Competitors are the ones a buyer in that city actually compares. Name them only when you know them.

Sample: a ₹299 snack, contents unknown. The ladder is one rung, ₹299. The shelf is `Missing`, so the sequence stops at fame.

## Video

- One job: fame, consideration, or a response.
- Three hooks in the audience language. They share a film and change the first line.
- Timed script from `assets/script.md`, inside `assets/kit.md` when the job is a campaign.
- End card: price, city, and the next action the business can fulfil.
- Say what you will not shoot.

Sample: two cities, one structure, the first line names the city. The pack is whatever the brief showed. No second pack is designed.

## Performance

The test, the kill rule, and COD sit in `performance.md`.

- One offer, one next step. A WhatsApp thread is `whatsapp.md`.
- Test hooks before you test audiences.
- Do not invent a ROAS, a CPA, or a conversion rate.
- Measure what the business can see.

Sample: two city cuts, three hooks, a kill rule labeled `Assumption:` because the brief had no account data.

## Creators

The sheet you send sits in `creators.md`. Fee bands sit in `benchmarks.md`.

- Language, city, and average views before follower count.
- Disclosure and the may-not-say list are in the brief.
- Whitelisting is in writing before a boost.

Sample: no creator is named, so the sheet's name is `Missing` and no fee is picked beyond the published band for a tier you do not know. Say the tier is `Missing`.

## B2B and company

Read `sectors/b2b.md`. The GST altitude is in `examples.md`.

- Who signs, who uses it, who can block it.
- Proof the brief contains. The tool they will not rip out, if the brief names it.
- Price in INR. "Prepare" is not "we file" unless the brief says so.

Sample: Indian CA firms, no city. Draft the desk. Any example city starts with `Assumption:`.

## Idea and brand platform

The shape sits in `platforms.md`.

- One tension. One line. Three places it shows up. What it will not say.
- A Mumbai-English platform is labeled as that.

Sample: the gift that has to look adequate and still be a price the host can repeat. The brand name is `Missing`, so the line does not go on a pack.
