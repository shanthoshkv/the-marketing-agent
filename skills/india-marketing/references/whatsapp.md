# WhatsApp

WhatsApp is a conversation the business has to answer. Consent and the opt-out are in `compliance.md`. Do not draft a broadcast for people who have not opted in.

Write the thread in the audience language, with an English working translation under it. Fill the brackets from the brief. If a bracket is unknown, write `Missing` there. Do not invent a name, a pin, or a delivery hour.

## First reply, after a click

> आपने [product] के बारे में पूछा था। कीमत [price] है। [city] में अभी [path] से मिलता है। बंद करने के लिए STOP लिखें।

English: You asked about [product]. The price is [price]. In [city] it is available through [path]. Reply STOP to end these notes.

If the path or the price is `Missing`, say so in the reply. Do not invent a shop.

## Catalogue

One line for what it is, one line for the price, one line for who it is for. No claim that `compliance.md` would cut. The pack photo is the real pack.

## Delivery update

Send this only when the business can name the day.

> [name], ऑर्डर [day] को निकलेगा। पिन [pin]। सवाल हो तो यहीं लिखें।

English: [name], the order leaves on [day]. Pin [pin]. Write here if you have a question.

## Filing-week note, CA firms

English is the right language until the firm asks for another. Send it only to firms that opted in.

> Filing week note for [firm]. We help prepare bills. We do not file the return. Reply with a time and the name of the person on your team who will use it. Reply STOP to end these notes.

## What you do not send

A voice note that hides the disclosure. A forward the viewer cannot tell is an ad. A message to a bought list. A medical claim. A "last 3 units" line unless the brief shows the stock.
